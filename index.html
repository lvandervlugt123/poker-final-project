How to Play Texas Hold'em

  • Each player gets 2 private hole cards.
  • Betting rounds: Preflop, Flop (3 cards), Turn (1 card), River (1 card).
  • Actions: Fold (give up), Check (bet 0), Call (match current bet), Raise (increase bet).
  • Preflop action starts left of Big Blind. Postflop action starts left of Dealer.
  • At showdown, best 5-card hand using any combo of hole + board wins the pot.
  • Hand ranking (high to low): Royal Flush, Straight Flush, Four of a Kind, Full House, Flush, Straight, Trips, Two Pair, Pair, High Card.
